Privacy Policy
==============

DailyRhythm is a local-first journal. Everything you log stays on your
device unless you choose to send it somewhere. There are no accounts, no
ads, no analytics and no DailyRhythm servers.

What the app stores
-------------------

On your device only:

* Moods, moments and tags you log.
* Meals, food items, prices, calories and health tags.
* Exercises, sleep, steps, weight and other health numbers you enter or import.
* Your optional profile (birth year, height, weight, sex), used only to
  show reference ranges.
* App settings.

Health Connect
--------------

If you turn on sync in Settings, DailyRhythm asks Health Connect for
**read-only** access to:

* Steps
* Sleep
* Resting heart rate
* Exercise sessions, with their distance and calories burned
* Past data (history), so a first sync can fill earlier days

This data is used only to show your days, metrics and garden inside the
app. It is stored on your device, never written back to Health Connect,
never sent to DailyRhythm or any third party, and never used for
advertising. You can revoke access at any time in Health Connect.

DailyRhythm's use of information received from Health Connect adheres to
the `Health Connect Permissions policy
<https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/12991134>`_,
including the Limited Use requirements.

What leaves your device, and only when you ask
----------------------------------------------

* **Google Drive backup.** If you sign in with Google and back up, a copy
  of your database is saved to a ``DailyRhythm_Backups`` folder in *your*
  Google Drive. The app can only see files it created there.
* **Calorie lookup.** When you tap "Look up" on a food, only the food's
  name is sent to `Open Food Facts <https://world.openfoodfacts.org>`_ to
  search for calories.
* **Export.** Files you export go wherever you save or share them.

Nothing is sent in the background.

Children
--------

DailyRhythm is not directed at children under 13.
